Latviski

  1. Nolasi mērķa formu uzdevuma kartītē.
  2. Uzklikšķini galotni vai ievelc to pie celma.
  3. Vari arī ierakstīt pilno formu un spiest Enter.
  4. “Rādīt noteikumu” atver locīšanas tabulu.

English

  1. Read the target form on the exercise card.
  2. Tap an ending or drag it next to the stem.
  3. You can also type the full form and press Enter.
  4. “Show rule” opens the declension table.
